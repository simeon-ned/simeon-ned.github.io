Simeon Nedelchev

I am a Researcher and Senior RL Engineer at the Institute of Artificial Intelligence, MIPT, and Senior Lecturer at Innopolis University, where I teach robotics and advise students on dynamics, control, and simulation.

With a decade of experience spanning research labs, startups, industry, and big tech, I build control for articulated robots — from novel actuators, modeling, and adaptive methods to legged locomotion, reinforcement learning, whole-body control, and sim-to-real deployment. Completed Ph.D. studies in Computer Science (Robotics) at Innopolis University; M.Sc. degrees from Korea University of Technology and Education and STANKIN.

Recent Projects

WBC-Mjlab: Whole Body Control in MuJoCo Lab

One shared MDP for whole-body motion tracking in mjlab — train once on a motion library, deploy one policy for many skills. Multi-clip training, paper-specific task presets, and sim-to-real export.

[Demo] [Code] [Docs]

PSM: Predictive Style Matching for Natural Locomotion

Natural and robust humanoid locomotion via an offline predictor that maps lower-body state and velocity commands to upper-body and gait targets during RL training — preserving task-only deployment on Unitree G1.

Simeon Nedelchev, Ekaterina Chaikovskaia, Egor Davydenko, Eduard Zaliaev, Roman Gorbachev · 2026 preprint

[Project page] [Paper] [Code]

LocoGen: Command-Conditioned Locomotion Generation

Combines learned motion matching and flow matching for online, command-conditioned full-body locomotion. Built for Unitree G1 but portable across robots: MuJoCo-grounded state, velocity commands (extensible to waypoints and trajectories), and interactive Viser control with seamless stand / walk / run transitions. Inference at ~800 FPS (LMM) and ~150 FPS (flow); PoC training from a handful of LAFAN clips. Walking and tracking validated with BeyondMimic / ZEST in simulation and on hardware.

Ongoing · code not yet published

Locomotion and Whole Body Control for Sber Green

Developer of the RL locomotion and whole-body control policies demonstrated on the Green humanoid at AIJ 2025: locomotion with improved naturalness and human-likeness; RL-based whole-body control trained on synthetically generated reference trajectories — without acquiring mocap datasets or retargeting — with a unified tracking policy for teleoperation, dance, and zero-shot skills.
